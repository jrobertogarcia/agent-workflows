# Root Cause Analysis

Objective investigation to identify the definitive trigger of an issue using evidence-based reasoning.

## Mission
Uncover the "How" and "Why" of the problem. Do not propose fixes or plans until the root cause is confirmed by evidence.

## Principles
- **Zero Assumptions**: Validate the entire logic path from entry to failure.
- **Evidence-First**: Support every conclusion with code, logs, or state observations.
- **Deep Trace**: Look beyond the symptom to find the systemic cause.

## Outcome
Identify the **Definitive Root Cause** and the **Evidence Chain** leading to it. Wait for user approval before proceeding to any solution.
